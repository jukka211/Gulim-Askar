Title:
An ongoing series of fashion illustrations
Material:
Ink, oil pastel, water colors
Year:
2024
Step into a world where fashion is more than just clothing, it's a form of expression. The ongoing series of fashion illustrations captures the essence of mood, attitude, and emotion through the art of style.
Each drawing tells a unique story, weaving together elements of design, color, and posture to create a visual narrative that is both captivating and timeless.
