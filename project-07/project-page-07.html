Title:
Martial Arts. Aikido.
Material:
Ink, oil pastel
Year:
2024
The series of illustrations captures the essence of Aikido in particular, a martial art that blends self-defense techniques with a deep philosophy of harmony and non-violence. Each illustration showcases a different Aikidoka practitioner in action, dynamically moving through the motions of their art in the dojo.
From sweeping throws to intricate joint locks, each illustration tells a story of strength, skill, and respect for their opponents. Whether practicing with a partner, or engaging in a lively sparring match, these illustrations convey the passion, fun and dedication of Aikido practitioners as they seek to perfect their art.
