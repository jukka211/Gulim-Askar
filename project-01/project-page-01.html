Project name:
Endless Summer!
Year:
2024
Endless Summer, wine, good food and flowers everywhere! Everything seems easier and nicer when it's summer! Food outside is tastier. Everything is opening up and there is even wine flowing and flowers blooming. Ink, oil pastel, 2024
As you know, with such a bountiful setting it is impossible not to praise the small moments of this wonderful life! The Summer will not end and will always remain in our hearts!
A series of illustrations inspired by summer moods from life and endless picnics.
