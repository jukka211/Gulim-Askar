Title:
Portraits of Wisdom
An ongoing series of portraits of people who have gathered life wisdom over the years. People who know exactly how to enjoy the moments of this short different, yet wonderful life.
Whether it's the unobtrusive dance of an old man or a trip to buy baguettes, we find that life is in fact lived in each small moment.

Ink, oil pastel
